Refund Policy

When an order can be cancelled, replaced, or refunded.

Last updated 25 October 2026. This policy applies to event production, garments, and souvenirs booked with Dreamers Den Event Management. Write to info@dreamersden.org with the order name, the issue, and photos where the goods are damaged or short. See also the Terms of Service.

Before production starts

A confirmed souvenir or garment order can be cancelled before cutting, printing, embroidery, or assembly starts. Amounts paid for goods not yet made are refunded. If blank materials were already purchased for that order, their cost is deducted and the rest is returned.

After production starts

Custom size, colour, branding, add-ons, and custom design make the goods specific to you. After production starts, a change of mind is not refundable. We still correct pieces that are defective, short, or damaged in transit.

  • Tell us within 7 days of delivery.
  • Include photographs of the defect, the shortage, or the packaging.
  • We repair or replace the affected pieces, or refund their value if a replacement is not practical.

Event bookings

  • If Dreamers Den cancels a confirmed service, fees paid for that cancelled portion are refunded.
  • If you cancel more than 14 days before the event date, fees for work not yet done are refunded. Costs already incurred stay with the booking. That includes booked crew, printed branding, and souvenirs already in production.
  • Inside 14 days, the confirmation states which amounts are retained. Deposits covering work already underway are not refunded.

How a refund is paid

Agreed refunds go back by the original payment method within 14 business days of the agreement. If that method cannot receive the refund, we agree another method with you in writing.
