Quotes and confirmation

The confirmation states the deposit, the balance, and when each is due. Souvenirs are delivered to the address or venue named in the order. Risk in finished goods passes on delivery. Tell us promptly if a shipment arrives short or damaged so we can follow the refund and replacement steps.
